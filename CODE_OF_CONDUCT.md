# Code of Conduct

This is a short code of conduct adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming good faith.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@jigardafda](https://github.com/jigardafda), privately through GitHub. For anything involving the security of the software, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps in deciding consequences: a private **correction** and clarification for unprofessional behaviour; a **warning** with consequences for continued behaviour; a **temporary ban** from interaction for serious violations; and a **permanent ban** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward others.
